# R7 targeted error audit — 14 October 2026

## Decision

R7's **official public score is 0.956** ([receipt](../r7_refinement_20261013/official_r7_score.json)). The strongest testable hypothesis for a *new* reader is an **anatomically constrained, cross-plane cartilage-lesion reader for Lateral and PF OA**, with an explicit report-label uncertainty audit before training. A synovitis-specific reader is a conditional second branch: it needs adjudicated synovial-thickening versus fluid labels and sequence checks first. Neither architecture has been trained or shown to improve R7.

The error numbers below are from **Gold58**, an expert-labeled set that this project has repeatedly used for development. R7's checkpoints reportedly excluded Gold58 from gradient training, but repeated use still makes it a diagnostic set, not an independent model-selection set. Pair counts share studies and cannot be treated as independent observations or an expected leaderboard gain.

## Exact local evidence

Inputs and hashes are recorded in [error_detail.json](error_detail.json); code is [audit_pairs.py](audit_pairs.py). It aligned 58 unique study IDs and all 12 binary labels, checked that `data/train.csv` matches the expert-label table for these IDs, and rejected nonfinite predictions. The R7 final, R7 compact, original CoAtNet, old ConvNeXt, old compact, and Candidate C predictions are all saved local outputs. R7 is a 50/50 percentile-rank blend of **the reused CoAtNet** and a new four-fold ConvNeXt-Small compact reader. Its [manifest](../r7_refinement_20261013/r7_manifest.json) states that the new reader has spatial attention, OA extent loss, synovitis decomposition, pair-ranking loss, and fluid/state readouts. These are manifest/source properties; the original remote training run has not been independently reproduced here.

The shared OA errors are concentrated in expert-positive studies: the top three account for **69/84 Lateral OA** and **63/82 PF OA** R7∩C wrong pairs. For synovitis, the top three positives account for **53/119**; shared errors span 24 positives and 23 negatives. A broad synovitis failure mechanism is more plausible than a single exceptional case. No patient IDs or report text are written into the generated outputs.

One leading Lateral OA expert-positive case has 39 R7∩C wrong pairs and its written report says *“Cartilages normal.”* The leading PF OA case has 35 shared wrong pairs; its available report describes a medial osteochondral fracture and about 9 mm of cartilage thinning, rather than clear PF compartment high-grade loss. A leading synovitis expert-positive case has 23 shared wrong pairs and its report describes Hoffa fat-pad impingement rather than explicit synovitis. These examples support **report/image-label discrepancy as a hypothesis**; they do not prove that the expert labels or reports are erroneous. Multilingual reports further complicate text extraction. Previous [label audit](../next_gain_20261012/oa_label_audit.json) found one label table that copied all 58 expert labels and excluded it from report-label comparisons; the exact R7 training-target CSV is not in this workspace.

The public R7 [state stacker](assets/state_stacker.json) is an important negative control. On the author's cross-fitted **weak-report** “explicit” ruler, added state readouts changed AUC by **−0.00620 Lateral OA, −0.00464 PF OA, −0.01020 Synovitis**; the author's own adoption rule dropped all three. A state-head or probability-calibration rerun is therefore an especially weak next bet. These author OOF metrics are label-proxy evidence, not independent clinical validation.

## Targeted reader A: cartilage-lesion continuity, without the failed mask dependency

The organizer defines OA by **greater than 50% cartilage-thickness loss over roughly 1 cm or more**, separately by compartment ([organizer definitions](https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/rsna-knee-abnormality-detection/discussion/733343)). R7's learned window extent and attention can capture correlates, but they do not directly enforce compartment identity or a contiguous physical-size lesion. The distinguishing signal to test is **localized cartilage-surface damage that persists across adjacent positions and is corroborated in another plane**.

Use reliable bony/joint landmarks and DICOM physical coordinates to place *broad* lateral femorotibial and patellofemoral view boxes; do not depend on the current SKM-TEA cartilage masks. The six-study transfer pilot [failed](../next_gain_20261012/kaggle_cartilage_mask_pilot/analysis.json): patellar cartilage coverage passed only 2/6, including none of three selected PF OA cases. Sample native-resolution 2.5D windows from lateral sagittal+coronal series and PF axial+sagittal series, preserving millimeter spacing. A small shared image encoder yields per-window lesion scores; a **contiguous-run or local top-k multiple-instance aggregator** combines neighboring windows within the named compartment. A second-plane agreement feature is an ablation, not an assumed requirement. Include a missing-plane mask and an unconditional R7 prediction as a frozen control.

First test **label policy versus architecture separately**. Under identical folds and reader architecture, compare the existing report targets with a prespecified policy that masks no cartilage detail/ambiguous high-grade size, keeps low-grade as negative, and retains explicit large high-grade as positive. Then, holding the better justified label policy fixed, compare: (1) R7-style broad views/attention control; (2) broad landmark boxes + contiguous aggregator; (3) the same without cross-plane agreement. If there are no trustworthy image-adjudicated validation labels, a weak-report OOF gain is only a proxy and cannot establish correction of the worst expert-positive cases.

The initial engineering gates are a 20-study landmark/crop QA including both OA classes, native-pixel and laterality checks, missing-series behavior, GPU memory, and p95 decode+inference time. The prior 24-study header-only pilot found complete geometric metadata across 129 series, but did **not** validate pixel alignment or lesion localization ([receipt](../next_gain_20261012/kaggle_geometry_pilot/output_v2/oa_geometry_pilot/receipt.json)). If broad boxes repeatedly miss the relevant cartilage or hide lesions, stop this architecture before full training.

## Targeted reader B: synovial-lining morphology, only with usable labels

R7 already includes a synovitis decomposition and fluid stacker. A second reader would need to measure a different image feature: **thick/irregular synovial lining in the suprapatellar recess and Hoffa/fat-pad regions while controlling for fluid volume**, rather than simply learning “bright fluid.” Small anatomical-box 2.5D branches with a fluid-volume covariate would be the minimal test. The matched control receives the same sequences and supervision but only fluid-volume/global features; if the morphology branch does not beat it under group-held-out validation, reject. Do not treat report mention of fat-pad inflammation as automatically equivalent to the challenge's synovitis label.

The sequence inventory currently records plane, fluid sensitivity, and fat suppression, **not contrast status** (`data/train_series.csv`). Noncontrast MRI can make synovium hard to separate from effusion; published knee MRI work describes this limitation ([MOST contrast MRI study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3885343/), [quantitative noncontrast DESS study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8591148/)). A [local grouped-manifest inventory](grouped_data_inventory.json) found only 564 synovitis weak targets at or above 0.6 and 125 at or below 0.15; the median is 0.3025, leaving a large ambiguous middle. These thresholds are descriptive and must be fixed before any supervised test. First establish which sequences actually exist in representative cases, whether synovitis and effusion can be adjudicated separately, and whether adequate, *confident* positive and negative groups exist. If these checks fail, do not launch the reader.

## Validation and advancement contract

1. Freeze a **fivefold patient/duplicate/report-group split** before modeling. The available [Phase 5 training manifest inventory](grouped_data_inventory.json) has 4,407 studies, 4,072 duplicate/report-linked groups, five folds, and 58 rows marked `expert_reused`. Its row keys include `patient_group`, `linked_report_cluster`, `expert_reused`, `label_source`, target values, and confidence. Audit R7's 4,344-study pool against this map; do not assume identical membership. Put every cache duplicate, repeated report, and patient across the same fold. The Phase 5 protocol listed only two `supervised_exclusion_uids`, **not all 58 Gold cases**; its OOF on Gold58 is therefore not independent clinical evidence. For the new test, exclude Gold58 and any other image-adjudicated evaluation cohort from *all* training, pseudo-labeling, teacher fitting, and checkpoint selection.
2. Produce **fold-exclusive** base R7 and new-reader predictions for the same held-out groups before any supervised blend. The public R7 asset bundle contains weights and stackers/specification, but no OOF prediction files. Its full checkpoint ensemble was trained on the non-Gold pool; evaluating a stacker on its predictions for that pool would leak. If R7 fold models or exact fold assignments cannot be reconstructed, use the new reader's matched architecture controls for weak-report OOF, and reserve complete R7 blending for a genuinely untouched image-labeled cohort or one frozen exploratory Kaggle run.
3. Report Lateral OA, PF OA, and Synovitis AUC separately, plus all 12-class macro AUC when a valid out-of-sample R7 reference exists. Record pair corrections and regressions with ties worth half credit, concentrated-case burden, and subgroup performance by report state, plane availability, laterality, scanner/spacing, and language where counts permit. Bootstrap **groups**, not positive-negative pairs; show five fold estimates and intervals. Do not optimize weights on Gold58 or leaderboard feedback.
4. Advance a reader only if the matched architecture contrast has a positive paired group-bootstrap lower bound on its target OOF AUC, positive direction in at least four folds, no material acquisition-stratum collapse, and demonstrated **incremental gain over out-of-sample R7** on an independent image-adjudicated set. Weak-report OOF alone is a feasibility/label-proxy gate, not a clinical accuracy gate. Require all 12 classes and a frozen blend to be checked before one official submission.
5. After a single-fold feasibility run, extrapolate measured p95 per-study inference to the hidden-test workload with a safety margin under Kaggle's runtime limit. Reject or simplify if decode, missing-plane fallback, memory, or full-test latency fail. Preserve exact R7 and Candidate C outputs and checkpoints as fallbacks.

**Current recommendation:** run the report-state/target provenance and 20-study image-box QA first. There is **no evidence yet** that either proposed reader increases 0.956, and the existing failed cartilage-mask pilot rules out using those masks as the shortcut.
